Common Questions Before Subscribing

How much does Album Download cost and can I cancel immediately?

A subscription costs $3.00 USD for 6 months of unlimited album downloads across Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. You can cancel immediately after subscribing with 1 click in the Stripe Customer Portal and still keep full access for the entire 6-month period.

Does it download full-resolution photos, videos, and Shared Albums?

Yes! Album Download downloads your photos and videos at original resolution in the exact visual order of your album, and works on both personal albums and Google Photos Shared Albums.
