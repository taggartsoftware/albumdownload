Subscription

How to purchase, activate, manage, cancel, or renew your Album Download subscription.

How to Purchase & Activate Your Subscription

Payments and subscription plans for Album Download are managed by Stripe.

Step 1: Click the Subscription Link in the Extension

After you install Album Download and sign in with your Google account, click on the link titled: "Click here to buy a subscription. It is required to download.":

Step 2: Complete Checkout via Stripe

Clicking the link opens a new window to begin checkout:

Step 3: Checkout Confirmation

If checkout is successful, it redirects to the confirmation page:

Step 4: Refresh the Album Download Tab

Important: Reload the Extension Tab After Checkout

If you then refresh the Album Download page, the warning will be gone and you will be able to download immediately!

How to Manage, Cancel, or Renew Your Subscription

To manage your subscription plan, select the "Manage Subscription" menu item inside the Album Download extension:

Canceling Your Plan Anytime

You can cancel your subscription plan at any time through the Stripe Customer Portal:

You will still be able to download until the end of the billing period, but the plan will not automatically renew:

It will show the exact date that it will be cancelled:

Renewing a Cancelled Plan

You can click "Renew plan" if you no longer wish for it to cancel:
