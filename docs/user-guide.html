User Guide

Detailed overview of Album Download features, filename templates, resize options, and bulk download controls.

Preserve Exact Album Order

Album Download captures every photo and video in the exact sequence shown on the Google Photos album page and numbers files sequentially (001, 002, 003…) so folders, slideshows, digital photo frames, and print books stay in order when sorted by Name (A–Z).

Album Subfolder & Custom Filename Templates

By default, Save as is set to {{title}}/{{title}} {{index3}}, which organizes each album into its own subfolder inside your browser's Downloads folder with a live filename preview before downloading. File extensions are added automatically.

  • {{title}} — Album title (for example, Family Trip). Remove {{title}}/ to save files directly into Downloads without a subfolder.
  • {{index}} — Unpadded item number (1, 2, 3…).
  • {{index2}} — 2-digit zero-padded item number (01, 02…).
  • {{index3}} — 3-digit zero-padded item number (001, 002…).
  • {{index4}} — 4-digit zero-padded item number (0001, 0002…), recommended for albums with 1,000 or more items.
  • {{index5}} — 5-digit zero-padded item number (00001, 00002…).

Personal & Shared Albums

Works on both your own albums (https://photos.google.com/album/...) and shared link albums from family, friends, clients, or events (https://photos.google.com/share/...).

Original-Size Photos & Videos

Downloads original-resolution photos and videos directly from Google's servers in their native formats (.jpg, .png, .heic, .heif, .webp, .mp4, .mov), preserving camera EXIF metadata.

Specify Size & Cropping

Enter Max width and/or Max height to scale photos down for digital photo frames, web galleries, or slideshows, or check Crop (with both Max width and Max height set) to crop photos to exact dimensions. Leave blank to download untouched originals.

Pause, Resume & Concurrent Downloads

Handles large 1,000+ item albums with a live completed/total progress counter, Pause and Resume controls, and adjustable concurrency (1 to 6 simultaneous downloads via the Settings gear).
