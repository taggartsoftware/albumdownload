Subscriptions, Billing & Cancellation

A paid subscription (processed by Stripe) is required to download albums with Album Download. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled prior to the renewal date.

You may cancel your subscription at any time using the Manage Subscription link inside the Album Download extension (see Subscription). When you cancel, your access remains active through the end of your current paid billing period.
